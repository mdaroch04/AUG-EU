DESTINATION GUIDE
What to expect at each stop
🇮🇹 Italy · Days 1–2 & 28–30
Milan
2 nights arrival · Returns Day 28 — 2-night farewell with proper buffer
Italy's fashion and design capital. A city of grand architecture, vibrant canal-side nightlife, and world-class art. Your trip begins and ends here.
🥾 Rooftop Duomo walk 🏛️ Last Supper (book early!) 🏰 Sforzesco Castle 🍹 Navigli aperitivo
🇮🇹 Italy · Days 3–8
The Dolomites
6 nights · Bolzano base + Cortina base · Rental car required
The highlight of the entire trip. Jaw-dropping pink-tinged rock spires, emerald lakes, flower-covered meadows and rifugio culture. Give this the time it deserves.
🥾 Tre Cime di Lavaredo circuit 🥾 Alpe di Siusi plateau 🥾 Cinque Torri trail 💙 Lago di Braies 🍽️ Rifugio lunch stops
🇦🇹 Austria · Days 9–10
Innsbruck
2 nights · Car → train handoff point
A stunning alpine city nestled in the Inn Valley with peaks rising directly above the old town. The Nordkette cable car launches you from the city centre to 2,200m.
🥾 Nordkette ridge walk (2,200m) 🏛️ Golden Roof old town 🎿 Bergisel ski jump tower 🥩 Tyrolean gröstl dinner
🇦🇹 Austria · Days 11–12
Grossglockner & Hohe Tauern
2 nights · Heiligenblut base
Austria's highest peak at 3,798m and Europe's most spectacular mountain toll road. The Pasterze is Austria's largest glacier — accessible and breathtaking even for beginners.
🥾 Pasterze Glacier walk 🛣️ High Alpine Road (€38/car) 🏔️ Franz-Josefs-Höhe viewpoint 🌸 Hohe Tauern wildflower walks
🇦🇹 Austria · Day 13
Salzburg
1 night · Return car here, switch to train
Mozart's birthplace and one of the world's most beautiful baroque cities. One day is genuinely enough to hit the highlights — then onwards to Bavaria.
🏰 Hohensalzburg Fortress 🌸 Mirabell Palace & Gardens 🍺 Augustinerbräu beer hall 🎵 Getreidegasse & old town
🇩🇪 Germany · Days 14–17
Munich + Eibsee + Walchensee
4 nights · Munich base · Both alpine lakes as day trips by Bayern Ticket
Bavaria at its best. Munich for culture and beer halls, then two of Germany's most beautiful mountain lakes — Eibsee under the Zugspitze, and quieter Walchensee for swimming and ridge views. Both done as day trips from Munich, keeping logistics simple.
🏛️ Marienplatz & English Garden 🥾 Eibsee lake circuit (7 km) 🥾 Herzogstand summit (2.5 hrs) 🏔️ Zugspitze cable car
🇨🇭 Switzerland · Days 18–19
Grindelwald + Zürich
1 night Grindelwald · 1 night Zürich transit en route to Ljubljana
The Eiger's north face looms over Grindelwald — one night is right for this trip. After the Bachalpsee hike, an afternoon train to Zürich breaks the journey to Ljubljana into two manageable legs and adds a bonus evening in one of Europe's most beautiful lakeside cities.
🥾 Bachalpsee alpine lake hike 🧗 First Cliff Walk ⛰️ Eiger, Mönch & Jungfrau views 🏙️ Zürich old town & lake
🇸🇮 Slovenia · Days 20–21
Ljubljana
2 nights · One of Europe's most underrated capitals
Small, walkable, affordable and genuinely lovely. Ljubljana has a fairy-tale old town, an easy castle walk, great outdoor cafe culture along the Ljubljanica river, and food that punches far above its price.
🥾 Castle forest walk (free) 🐉 Dragon Bridge & Triple Bridge 🚣 Kayaking on Ljubljanica 🍷 Metelkova arts & culture quarter
🇸🇮 Slovenia · Days 23–25
Triglav NP — Bled & Bohinj
3 nights · Bled 2 nights + Bohinj 1 night recommended
Arguably the most beautiful stop on the trip. Lake Bled with its island church is iconic — but don't miss Bohinj, which is wilder, emptier and even more stunning. Go early; August crowds are real.
🥾 Vintgar Gorge boardwalk 🥾 Savica Waterfall (1 hr) 🏰 Bled Castle viewpoint 🍰 Kremšnita cream cake
🇮🇹 Italy · Day 26
Venice
1 night · Stay in Mestre (mainland) to save money
One day is exactly right for this trip. Venice in August is hot, crowded and expensive on the island — stay in Mestre, commute in, and plan around early mornings and late evenings.
🚢 Vaporetto Line 1 canal ride 🐟 Rialto market morning 🏛️ Doge's Palace (pre-book) 🍷 Cicchetti & ombra in a bacaro
🇮🇹 Italy · Day 27
Verona
1 night · Tightened from 2 nights — all highlights fit in one day
A perfectly-sized Roman city with one of the world's best-preserved amphitheatres. One full day covers everything comfortably — the extra night has been returned to Milan as a proper farewell buffer day.
🏛️ Arena di Verona 🏟️ Piazza delle Erbe & tower 💌 Juliet's House & Castelvecchio 🎭 Opera in August (from €30)

Alpe di Siusi Plateau
EasyFull Day
Dolomites, Italy · Day 4
Cable car from Ortisei to Europe's largest high-altitude plateau. Gentle meadow paths with jaw-dropping Dolomite peaks in every direction.
8–12 kmDistance round trip
~200 mElevation plateau only
4–6 hrsDuration incl. cable car
💶 Seiser Alm Bahn cable car — ~€24pp return · buy online or at station
🧭Walk west from Compatsch toward Saltria and Sassolungo — puts the most dramatic peaks in front of you throughout.
🚡The Bullaccia/Puflatsch chairlift from Compatsch adds a higher vantage point over the plateau — worth taking if open.
🕗Avoid descending by gondola 12–2pm — queues build to 30–45 min. Descend before 11:30am or after 3pm.
🍽️Pre-book a rifugio lunch table at Rifugio Compatsch or Rifugio Ortisei — walk-ins in August can mean 45-min waits.
⚠️Common mistake: driving toward the plateau. Cars are strictly prohibited. Park in Ortisei, walk 5 min to gondola base.
🧭 Follow the Compatsch → Saltria axis and use the Bullaccia chairlift — these two changes materially improve the scenic payoff of the plateau walk.
Driving & Parking
🅿️Parking: Ortisei (St. Ulrich) - Parkhaus Ortisei or Parkhaus Sochers. Paid. €10–15/day. No reservation needed but fills by 9am in August.
🚡Cable car: Seiser Alm Bahn from Ortisei. ~€22–25pp return. Runs from 8am. Buy at station or online.
🚫Road restriction: Driving onto the Alpe di Siusi plateau is strictly prohibited for non-residents. Cable car or bus is mandatory - no exceptions.
Timing & Trail Reality
🕗Best start: Catch the 8am first gondola. Plateau becomes very crowded 10am–2pm in August.
🔁Route: Flexible loop - no fixed trail. Navigate by rifugio names (Compatsch, Saltria, Goldknopf). AllTrails has several marked routes.
⏱️Total experience: 5–7 hrs including cable car queues, lunch at a rifugio, and the descent.
Practical Tips
🍽️Book a rifugio lunch table in advance (Rifugio Compatsch or Rifugio Ortisei). Unreserved walk-ins in August can mean a 45-min wait.
⚠️Do not take the gondola back down in the 12–2pm window - queues build to 30–45 min. Aim to descend by 11:30am or after 3pm.
📍Common mistake: Driving toward the plateau expecting to park at the top. You cannot. Park in Ortisei and walk to the gondola base station (5 min).
🔥 RECOMMENDED UPGRADE
Why this upgrades Alpe di Siusi
⭐Views: Alpe di Siusi looks across a plateau. Seceda puts you on a narrow ridge at 2,500m with the Odle/Geisler spires — 500m vertical fins of rock — directly in front of you. One of the most photographed landscapes in the Alps.
📍Same base: Seceda gondola departs from Ortisei — the same village as the Alpe di Siusi cable car. Zero rerouting required.
🗓️Best combined plan: Seceda gondola in the morning (8am) → ridge walk → return by noon → Alpe di Siusi plateau loop in the afternoon (2–3 hrs). Both done in the same day from the same car park.
Seceda Ridge Walk
EasyHalf Day
Ortisei, Val Gardena · Day 4 (combine with Alpe di Siusi)
Stand on a narrow grass ridge at 2,500m facing the Odle spires — a row of near-vertical 500m rock fins rising from the hillside. This is the quintessential Dolomites photograph, and it cannot be seen from the plateau below.
4–6 kmDistance round trip
~200 mElevation ridge undulation
3–4 hrsDuration incl. cable car
💶 Seceda gondola — ~€28pp return · 2 stages Ortisei → Furnes → Seceda
🚡Gondola: Seceda gondola from Ortisei (2 stages — village → Furnes → Seceda, 2,518m). ~€25–30pp return. Opens 8am. Book online or at station.
🧭Route: From the top station, walk the Rasciesa ridge southward toward Col Raiser. The Odle spires face you directly from the ridge. Return same way or descend via forest trail to Ortisei (30 min walk).
🕗Best timing: First gondola at 8am. The Odle spires catch morning light from the east — most dramatic before 10am. Crowds build significantly from 10:30am onward.
⚠️Same parking constraint as Alpe di Siusi — park at Ortisei Parkhaus, 5 min walk to both gondola bases. If doing both on Day 4, this is the ideal morning start.
🔥 If the group does only one activity on Day 4, Seceda is the stronger scenic experience. If doing both: Seceda 8–11am, Alpe di Siusi 12–4pm.
Tre Cime di Lavaredo
ModerateFull Day
Dolomites, Italy · Day 8
The crown jewel of the Dolomites. 9.5 km circuit around three iconic spires. Rocky alpine terrain, rifugio lunch, absolutely unforgettable.
9.5 kmDistance round trip
~500 mElevation round trip gain
4–5 hrsDuration with stops
🔄Go counterclockwise — the north face (the dramatic concave face) is reached early while legs are fresh, and morning light hits it directly.
🏔️At Forcella di Lavaredo (the saddle, ~2,454m, highest point): pause here for 20–30 min. Most groups rush through — this is the single best viewpoint on the circuit.
👀On the descent from Forcella back to Rifugio Auronzo: look behind you. The south face of the towers appears from this angle and most people miss it entirely while focusing on the path.
🍽️Rifugio Lavaredo for lunch (halfway, ~12:30pm arrival): book a table in advance for August. Walk-in queues can be 45+ min.
🧭 Counterclockwise + deliberate time at Forcella di Lavaredo transforms this from a great walk into a truly memorable one.
Driving & Parking
🅿️Parking: Rifugio Auronzo (2,333m) - paid toll booth on access road SP49. €30/vehicle, collected at the barrier. No separate parking fee at the top.
⏰Access restriction: In August, SP49 is typically restricted to private vehicles 9am–5pm. Outside those hours, access is free-flow. Verify current policy before August 2026 at comune.cortina.bl.it.
🚌If you miss the window: Shuttle buses run from Lago di Misurina (~5km away, free parking). ~€5pp each way. Add 20 min to your start.
📍Approach: From Cortina, take SS51 north then SS48 east toward Misurina. SP49 is the final toll access road. Total ~45 min from Cortina.
Timing & Trail Reality
🕕Best start: Leave Cortina by 6:30am. Arrive Rifugio Auronzo by 7:15–7:30am before the access restriction and before the lot fills (~200 spaces).
🔁Route: Loop - counterclockwise recommended. Best tower views face north/east, which gets morning light heading counterclockwise.
⏱️Total experience: 4–5 hrs including rifugio lunch stop, photo time at Forcella di Lavaredo, and descent back to the car park.
🏔️Key stop: Rifugio Lavaredo (halfway) for coffee/lunch. Forcella di Lavaredo saddle (the high point at ~2,454m) is the single best viewpoint.
August is the most crowded month of the year at Tre Cime. Arriving after 9am means shuttle buses, full parking, and narrow trails clogged with tour groups. This is non-negotiable: 6:30am departure.
Lago di Braies Circuit
EasyHalf Day
Dolomites, Italy · Day 7 (Fri, Aug 8) · Pre-booked parking essential
One of Europe's most beautiful mountain lakes — turquoise water, pale rock spires, and a famous red boathouse. The lake circuit is flat and accessible. Arrive before 7:15am or access is denied.
3.5 kmDistance round trip
~50 mElevation essentially flat
45–60 minDuration lake circuit only
📍At the far (north) end of the lake circuit, a trail climbs toward Rifugio Biella. The first 30–45 min of ascent (~150–200m gain) delivers an overhead perspective of the lake — the bird's-eye view that makes Braies famous in photographs.
⏱️Add 1–1.5 hrs to the morning. No need to reach the full rifugio — turn around when the view opens up (~150m above the lake).
💡The full Rifugio Biella ascent (900m, 3–4 hrs each way) is outside the scope of this trip. The partial extension is the right call.
🤏 Marginal improvement — worth doing only if the group feels fresh after the circuit and has an extra hour before the trailhead fills with coaches (by 9am).
Driving & Parking
⚠️Pre-booking mandatory: Parking must be reserved in advance at the official Lago di Braies parking system. In August, lots are fully allocated to pre-booked vehicles. Walk-ups are redirected to shuttle buses.
🕕Depart 6:15am from Cortina accommodation. Arrive at the lake by 7:15am to secure parking and beat the coach groups arriving from 8:30am onward.
🚌Shuttle alternative: If parking is full, a shuttle runs from Braies di Fuori (~3km away, free parking). Add 20 min to your timeline and book the shuttle in advance too.
Route & Timing
🔁Walk counterclockwise (right from the hotel when facing the lake). The Seekofel peak views are in front of you on the return leg when morning light is best.
🚣Rowing boats available from the dock near the hotel — 30 min on the water gives a unique perspective. Book on arrival (not in advance).
📸The classic postcard shot is from the south end, looking north across the lake toward the mountain. Achievable before 7:30am without other people in frame.
Pre-book parking well in advance — this is non-negotiable in August. Without a reservation you will be turned away at the barrier.
Cinque Torri Trail
Easy–ModHalf Day
Cortina, Italy · Day 6 (en route)
Five rock towers with WWI mountain trenches still intact. 3-hour loop with broad Dolomite views. Chairlift option available to cut the climb.
5.5 kmDistance round trip
~150 mElevation with chairlift
2–3 hrsDuration with chairlift
Driving & Parking
🅿️Parking: Large free car park at Passo Falzarego (2,105m), directly off SS48. No reservation. Fills by 10am in August - arrive by 9am at the latest as this is an en-route stop.
🚡Chairlift (Seggiovia Cinque Torri): Base at the Falzarego car park. ~€15–18pp return. Runs July–Sept from ~9am. Skips ~200m of ascent. Worth it for the time saving on this half-day stop.
📍Location: Directly on SS48 between Cortina and Arabba - zero detour required on Day 6 drive.
Timing & Trail Reality
🔁Route: Loop around the five towers. Trench museum (Museo all'Aperto) is integrated into the trail - no ticket, walk-in access.
⏱️Total experience: 2–3 hrs including chairlift, tower loop, and WWI trenches. Allow 30 min extra if exploring the museum sections thoroughly.
🕙Crowds: Coach groups arrive 10am–2pm. This is an en-route afternoon stop - timing naturally avoids the worst of it.
Practical Tips
📌The chairlift bottom and top stations are at the car park level - you walk to the towers from the lift top in 10 min. Without the lift, add 40 min ascent each way.
💡If short on time (e.g. running late on the drive): skip the chairlift, walk the base of the towers from the car park (2km, flat). Still worth a 45-min stop even without the full loop.
🔥 RECOMMENDED UPGRADE
Why this upgrades Cinque Torri
⭐Views: Cinque Torri keeps you at ground level among the rocks. Lagazuoi puts you at 2,778m with a 360° panorama — Marmolada, Tofane, Pelmo, Civetta, and the Cinque Torri visible directly below you.
📍Same car park: The Lagazuoi cable car base is 150m from the Cinque Torri chairlift base at Passo Falzarego. You are already there.
⛏️WWI tunnels: Lagazuoi was a major WWI front. Military tunnels carved through the mountain can be descended on foot (1 hr, brings you back to the car park) — more compelling historically than the Cinque Torri trenches.
Lagazuoi Summit
Cable Car1.5–2 hrs

0 kmHiking cable car to summit
2,778 mSummit elevation
1.5–2 hrsDuration incl. tunnel descent
💶 Lagazuoi cable car — ~€18pp return · 6 min ride to 2,778m · no pre-booking
🚡Cable car: ~€17–20pp return (2026 est.). 6-minute ride. No pre-booking required but can queue in August — go first thing.
⛏️Tunnel descent: From the summit, descend through 1 km of original WWI military tunnels back to the car park (included in cable car ticket). Bring a headlamp or phone torch. 45–60 min descent. Optional — you can take the cable car both ways.

Nordkette Ridge Walk
EasyHalf Day
Innsbruck, Austria · Day 10
Cable car from the city centre launches you to 2,334m. Short ridge hike above the clouds looking down on the Inn Valley and Innsbruck below.
4–5 kmDistance round trip
~430 mElevation net descent, ridge
2–3 hrsDuration walking only
💶 Nordkettenbahn full return (Congress–Hafelekar) — ~€45pp · book at nordkette.com
🧭Take cable car to Hafelekar (2,334m) → walk ridge toward Seegrube (1,905m) — this is the descent direction, with Innsbruck laid out below you throughout.
🏔️Kaminspitze (2,470m): a 20–30 min scramble from Hafelekar adds ~150m gain and a wider Stubai Alps panorama. Suitable only if the ridge walk already feels comfortable — some loose rock and exposure. Marginal improvement, not essential.
☁️If weather is overcast or windy at Hafelekar: descend to Seegrube (1,905m) and walk the plateau area there instead. Still worth the trip — views of Innsbruck are clearer from Seegrube on hazy days.
⏱️Be back in Innsbruck by 1–2pm — Minivan #2 pickup is this afternoon and it needs to be in-hand before 3pm for the early Day 11 departure.
🧭 Walk Hafelekar → Seegrube (not the reverse). The city view is in front of you the entire time, and it's a descent rather than a climb after the cable car.
Access Without Car
🚡Cable car system (Nordkettenbahn): 3 stages from Innsbruck old town - Hungerburgbahn funicular (Congress station) → Seegrube (1,905m) → Hafelekar (2,334m).
🎫Tickets: Full return Congress–Hafelekar ~€42–48pp (2026 est.). Book online via nordkette.com - August queues at the valley station are significant. First car departs ~7:30am.
🚶Getting to Congress station: 5-min walk from Innsbruck Altstadt. No transport needed.
🕗Recommended: Take the first or second gondola (7:30–8:30am). Ridge is noticeably less crowded before 10am.
Timing & Trail Reality
🔁Route: Take cable car to Hafelekar (2,334m) → walk the rocky ridge trail toward Seegrube (1,905m) → cable car back down. Net descent ~430m. Alternatively, turn around at a viewpoint and return same route.
⚠️Terrain note: The ridge walk between Hafelekar and Seegrube has exposed, rocky sections requiring hiking shoes. Some scrambling. Not technical, but not a paved path. Unsuitable in wet or windy conditions.
⏱️Total experience: 3–4 hrs including cable car rides (each stage ~8 min), summit time, and ridge walk.
🌤️Views: On a clear morning, you can see across the Inn Valley to the Stubai Alps. The view down to Innsbruck is the best in the region.
Practical Tips
📌Minivan #2 is picked up this afternoon - keep the morning free for the cable car. The Nordkette is a half-day activity. Plan to be back in Innsbruck by 1–2pm.
💡If the full ridge walk feels too exposed for the group: take the cable car to Seegrube only (~€30pp return), walk the easy Seegrube plateau area (1 hr, flat), and return. Still worth the trip.
Pasterze Glacier Walk
EasyHalf Day
Grossglockner, Austria · Day 12
Walk to the toe of Austria's largest glacier from Franz-Josefs-Höhe. Flat and accessible. The blue-white glacier landscape is extraordinary.
6 kmDistance round trip
~180 mElevation round trip gain
2–3 hrsDuration with funicular
🚗Edelweißspitze (2,577m) — reached by a 2-minute spur road off the main Grossglockner High Alpine Road, before you reach Franz-Josefs-Höhe. 5-minute walk from the car park to the summit cross.
⭐Views: 360° panorama over both north and south sides of the main Alpine divide. The Großglockner peak, the glacier, and the full chain of high-altitude peaks are visible from here — wider scope than Franz-Josefs-Höhe alone.
📍Many groups miss this entirely because the spur road looks minor on the map. It is not. Add it to the Day 11 drive south — it adds 15 minutes and zero hiking.
🏔️Fuscher Törl (2,428m): pull-off viewpoint on the main road with a small chapel. North-facing view across the Salzburg Alps — a different character from the glacier views to the south. Stop 10 min here on the way in.
🔄Return trip tip: The north-facing views on the way back to Innsbruck are completely different from the south-facing views on the way in. Stop at Fuscher Törl again on the return — same location, different light and perspective.
🔥 Edelweißspitze is a better panoramic viewpoint than the Franz-Josefs-Höhe terrace for zero extra hiking. It takes 15 minutes and is on your route. This is a must-do, not optional.
Driving & Parking
🛣️Access: Drive the Grossglockner High Alpine Road from Bruck/Fusch (north) to Franz-Josefs-Höhe. The €38/vehicle toll is paid at the Ferleiten booth - not separately at the parking area.
🅿️Parking: Franz-Josefs-Höhe car park (2,369m). Included in road toll. Large capacity. Busy 10am–3pm in August - arriving early (before 10am) means easy parking and shorter queues.
📍Drive time to Franz-Josefs-Höhe: ~1h 30m from Innsbruck via Bruck an der Glocknerstraße. Allow extra time at viewpoints en route.
Timing & Trail Reality
🕗Best start: Depart Innsbruck by 7:30am (Day 11) to arrive at Franz-Josefs-Höhe by 10am in morning light, before the Ferleiten toll queue builds.
🚡Gletscherbahn funicular: Small funicular at Franz-Josefs-Höhe descends to the glacier moraine level (~€5pp return). Saves ~150m of descent. Opens ~9am. Worth taking to get closer to the ice.
🔁Route options: (1) Gamsgrube nature trail loop (4km, ~180m, 1.5 hrs) - recommended. (2) Moränenweg (morraine path along glacier edge, ~3km, flat). Combine both for a full 6km experience.
⏱️Total experience at Franz-Josefs-Höhe: 2–3 hrs including funicular, walks, and viewpoint stops.
🧊Glacier reality: The Pasterze has retreated significantly in recent decades. You will see a large ice field but may need to walk 10–15 min along the moraine to reach the current ice margin.
Practical Tips
🌤️August afternoon thunderstorms are common in this area. Start early and aim to be back at the car by 2pm.
💡The view from the main Franz-Josefs-Höhe terrace (free, no hike required) is already spectacular. Even 30 min at the viewpoint with no walking is worthwhile if the group is tired from the road.
Eibsee Circuit
EasyHalf Day
Bavaria, Germany · Day 16
A 7 km loop around one of Germany's most beautiful glacial lakes, turquoise beneath the Zugspitze. Flat, easy, and stunning at every step.
7.2 kmDistance round trip
~80 mElevation essentially flat
2–2.5 hrsDuration lake only
💶 Zugspitze summit cable car — ~€67pp return · pre-book at zugspitze.de · Bayern Ticket covers train to Eibsee
Access Without Car
🚆Step 1: Munich Hbf → Garmisch-Partenkirchen. Bayern Ticket valid. ~1 hr 15 min, runs hourly. Depart Munich by 7:30am.
🚞Step 2: Zugspitzbahn (cogwheel train) from Garmisch station → Eibsee station. ~10 min. Included on Bayern Ticket. Alternatively: 20 min walk from Garmisch station.
🎫Zugspitze cable car (Eibsee-Seilbahn): Departs from Eibsee station. ~€65–70pp return (2026 est.). Pre-book at zugspitze.de - August walk-up queue is 2–3 hrs.
🚌Return: Zugspitzbahn from Eibsee back to Garmisch, then Bayern Ticket train to Munich.
Timing & Trail Reality
🕗Best start: Arrive Eibsee by 8:30–9am. The lake circuit is most beautiful with morning light on the Zugspitze.
🔁Route: Full loop clockwise (signposted "Eibseerundweg"). Flat throughout. Passes several hidden bays and inlets.
⏱️Total experience: 2.5 hrs (lake circuit) + 3–4 hrs (Zugspitze cable car + summit) = full day if combining both. Lake alone is 2–3 hrs.
🌊Swimming at the lake is allowed and popular in August (~18°C water). Best swimming spots on the north shore.
Practical Tips
⚠️Two different train systems at Garmisch: The Zugspitzbahn cogwheel (to Eibsee) departs from Garmisch main station, platform 4–5. The Eibsee cable car (to Zugspitze summit) is a separate system at Eibsee. Don't confuse the two.
💡If skipping the summit: the lake circuit + a swim is a perfect 3-hour morning. Take a late Bayern Ticket train back to Munich (~3pm) and still have an evening in the city.
Herzogstand Summit
ModerateHalf Day
Walchensee, Germany · Day 17
Climb above Walchensee to sweeping panoramic Alps views from 1,731m. Cable car option available. Swim in the lake when you return.
5 kmDistance with cable car
~350 mElevation with cable car
2.5–3.5 hrsDuration walking only
💶 Herzogstandbahn cable car — ~€20pp return · runs from 9am · Walchensee village
🧭After reaching Herzogstand summit (1,731m), continue along the ridge to Heimgarten (1,790m) — 2 km of sustained ridge walking with 360° views over Walchensee, Kochelsee, and the Bavarian plain.
📊Adds ~2 km and 60m elevation each way. Total on-foot time with traverse: 3.5–4.5 hrs vs 2.5–3.5 hrs for Herzogstand only.
🔄From Heimgarten, descend via the trail toward Walchensee lakeshore (different arrival point from the cable car base — short 15-min walk back to the village). Makes the full route a loop rather than an out-and-back.
⚠️Bus schedule constraint: with traverse, plan for a 5pm return bus at the latest. Cable car at 9am → traverse → lake swim by 3pm → bus 4–5pm to Munich. Tight but achievable.
🔥 The traverse turns a good summit into an excellent ridge experience. The difference between standing at Herzogstand and walking the full ridge to Heimgarten is substantial — 45 min of sustained panoramic views vs a single viewpoint. Recommended if the group is willing to shorten the lake swim.
Access Without Car
🚌Step 1: RVO bus 9622 from Munich (Starnberg/Pasing direction) → Kochel am See or Walchensee. ~1 hr 30 min. Check MVV timetable - service is limited (2–3 buses/day). Depart Munich by 8am.
🚡Step 2: Herzogstandbahn cable car from the Walchensee lakeshore (at Walchensee village). ~€18–22pp return. Runs from 9am, July–Oct.
🚌Return bus to Munich: Check exact times before the trip - last bus is typically 5–6pm. Missing it means a taxi to Kochel (~€20) + train home.
Timing & Trail Reality
📊Stats clarification: 8 km / 760 m is the FULL hike from the lake without cable car. With the cable car: ~5 km / ~350 m gain on foot (cable car top station ~1,580m → summit 1,731m → optional Heimgarten 1,790m).
🔁Route with cable car: Lift to Herzogstand Alm (~1,580m) → summit (1,731m) → optional: continue to Heimgarten (1,790m) via ridge → return same way or loop back to lift. Total on foot: 4–5 km.
⏱️Total experience: Bus travel (3 hrs round trip) + cable car + walk + lake swim = full day. Allow 5–6 hrs at the lake/mountain.
🏊Walchensee swimming: the water is clear and cold (~18–20°C in August). Best beach area is at the south end near Walchensee village.
Practical Tips
⚠️Critical: Confirm the return bus time before you go. The RVO 9622 schedule is thin. Screenshot the timetable offline.
💡If the cable car is closed or too expensive: the Walchensee lakeshore walk (6.5km flat loop) is a worthwhile alternative. Still a great day trip.
Bachalpsee Alpine Lake
Easy–ModHalf Day
Grindelwald, Switzerland · Day 19
Take the First gondola then walk 2.5 hrs to a stunning alpine lake that perfectly mirrors the Eiger, Mönch and Jungfrau. Unmissable.
7 kmDistance round trip
~200 mElevation gondola does the rest
3–4 hrsDuration incl. Cliff Walk
💶 Grindelwald First gondola — ~€61pp return · pre-book jungfrau.ch · includes First Cliff Walk
📍Walk to the far (north) end of Bachalpsee — most people stop at the south shore viewpoint. The classic mirror reflection of Eiger, Mönch and Jungfrau requires the north end, 10 additional minutes of walking.
🌊Wind picks up from late morning and kills the reflection effect. Reach the lake before 10am for calm water. The early gondola (8:30am opening) makes this achievable.
⏱️Day 19 constraint: back in Grindelwald by 12:30pm to catch a 1:30–2pm train to Zürich (2 hrs). Plan the gondola descent accordingly — do not extend the lake time beyond 11:30am.
🧭 The 10-minute extension to the north shore is the single most important execution detail for this hike — it's the difference between a good lake view and the iconic one.
Access Without Car
🚡Grindelwald First gondola: Departs Grindelwald terminal (5 min walk from village centre). ~€38–45pp one way, ~€58–65pp return (2026 est.). Book in advance at jungfrau.ch - August queues at the valley station are 30–60 min without a booking.
🕗First gondola opening: Approximately 8:30am in August. Arrive at the valley station by 8:00–8:15am to board on the first or second car.
📍From gondola top (First, 2,168m): Well-marked trail to Bachalpsee (2,265m). Approximately 3.5km each way. Clear path, no navigation required.
Timing & Trail Reality
🔁Route: Out-and-back from First station to Bachalpsee. The trail is wide gravel path, easy to follow. First Cliff Walk is a side-loop at the gondola top station (add 30 min, included in gondola ticket).
📊Elevation note: From First (2,168m) to Bachalpsee (2,265m) = 97m gain each way. ~200m total round trip. The card's 350m figure is inaccurate - the gondola removes virtually all the elevation challenge.
🏔️Best viewpoint: Walk to the far (north) end of the lake for the full mirror reflection of Eiger, Mönch and Jungfrau. The near end of the lake, while beautiful, gives an angled view.
⏱️Total experience: 3–4 hrs including gondola, Cliff Walk, lake walk, and time at Bachalpsee.
Practical Tips
💡This is Day 19 (Thu Aug 20) - the group departs for Zürich in the afternoon. Plan to be back at Grindelwald village by 12:30pm to catch a ~1:30–2pm train to Zürich (2 hrs).
⚠️One-way gondola down is cheaper if you prefer to walk down (3.5km, ~1 hr descent). Saves ~€25pp but adds time.
Vintgar Gorge Walk
EasyHalf Day
Bled, Slovenia · Day 24
A 1.6 km wooden boardwalk carved into the walls of a dramatic emerald river gorge. Easy, spectacular, one of Slovenia's most beloved walks.
3.2 kmDistance gorge only
~70 mElevation essentially flat
1.5–2 hrsDuration gorge walk
Access Without Car
🚌Option A (recommended): Seasonal shuttle bus from Bled bus station to Vintgar gorge entrance. ~€3pp each way, 15 min. Runs Jul–Sept, hourly from 8am.
🚶Option B: Walk from Bled centre through Podhom village (~4km, 45 min). Mostly flat road. Combine both ways for a round trip with variety.
🎫Entrance fee: ~€10pp adult (2026 est.). Pay at the ticket booth at the gorge entrance. No advance booking needed - just arrive early.
Timing & Trail Reality
🔁Route: Out-and-back along 1.6km of wooden boardwalk to Šum waterfall. Return the same path. Not a loop.
⏱️Total experience: 1.5–2 hrs in the gorge (board walk is slow due to crowds + photo stops). Add transit time: 2.5–3 hrs total from Bled.
🕗Best start: Arrive at the gorge entrance at opening time (8am). By 9:30am it is extremely crowded - narrow boardwalks become shoulder-to-shoulder. This is the most crowd-sensitive stop in Slovenia.
🌊The water in the gorge is a vivid emerald green from glacial minerals. Best colours in morning light.
Vintgar in peak August is genuinely unpleasant after 9:30am - tour coaches arrive in waves. The 8am opening is not just recommended, it is the difference between a magical experience and a queue.
Savica Waterfall
EasyHalf Day
Bohinj, Slovenia · Day 25
A beautiful 1-hour walk through forest to an impressive 78m waterfall. Combine with a wild swim in Lake Bohinj afterward — paradise in August.
3 kmDistance round trip incl. approach
~200 mElevation steep stone steps
1.5–2 hrsDuration waterfall & back
🚡Vogel cable car departs from Ribčev Laz (the main Bohinj bus stop). Ascends to 1,540m in 10 min. ~€20–25pp return (2026 est.).
⭐View: the best overview of the entire Bohinj basin — lake, valley, Julian Alps, and Triglav visible in the distance. The waterfall and lake walk don't give this perspective. This is the only way to see Bohinj from above without a serious hike.
🗓️Best sequence for Day 25: Savica waterfall (morning, 9am) → bus back to Ribčev Laz → lake swim (midday) → Vogel cable car (early afternoon, 2pm) → bus back to Bled by 5pm.
⏱️Easy walks at the Vogel top station (~1–2 hrs, flat ski area paths). Or just take the cable car for the view and come straight back down (30 min total).
🔥 The Bohinj day is currently the most passive day in Slovenia. Vogel converts it into the best overhead perspective of the entire trip's lake segment. Recommended if budget allows (~€20pp extra).
Access Without Car
🚌Step 1: Bus from Bled to Bohinj Jezero (Lake Bohinj). Line 8, runs multiple times/day, ~30 min, ~€4pp.
🚌Step 2: From Bohinj bus stop, walk ~2km to the Savica parking area (30 min flat road walk) or take a taxi (~€5, ask at Bohinj bus stop).
🎫Entrance fee: ~€3pp at the trail gate. No advance booking.
📍Trailhead: The Savica trail begins at the parking/gate area (1.5km past Ukanc village) and follows stone steps up to the waterfall viewpoint.
Timing & Trail Reality
📊Distance note: 3.5km covers the full round trip from the bus stop in Ukanc including the approach road. From the entrance gate to the waterfall: ~0.7km with 500+ stone steps. It's short but steep.
⏱️Total experience: 1.5–2 hrs for the waterfall. Budget 4–5 hrs for the full Bohinj day (bus travel + waterfall + swim in the lake).
🔁Route: Out-and-back. You do not walk behind the waterfall - the viewpoint is a ledge looking across at the 78m drop. Most dramatic in morning light.
🏊After Savica: Walk or bus back to the main Bohinj lake (Ribčev Laz). The lake is one of the cleanest in the Alps - excellent wild swimming in August (~20–22°C water).
Practical Tips
💡Combine with the lake on the same day (Day 25). Savica in the morning, lake swim in the afternoon, bus back to Bled by 5pm.
📌The stone steps are uneven and can be slippery if wet. Hiking shoes recommended; flip-flops will be uncomfortable on the ascent.

Venice — Mestre stay — already planned correctly. Island accommodation costs €50–100/night more. Do not change this call.
